TRADE JOURNAL
=============
A self-hosted trading journal for cTrader (prop-firms).
Runs in a single Docker container on your own machine - your data never
leaves it. Built around how scalpers actually trade: hotkey scale-in
entries and partial take-profits are grouped into ONE logical trade.


WHAT'S IN IT
------------

Dashboard
  Net P&L, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, avg R, drawdown, streaks,
  fees, best/worst days. Equity curve in $ or R (green in profit, red in
  drawdown), R-distribution, daily P&L calendar, time-of-day heatmap,
  and breakdowns by symbol / setup / session / weekday.

Live
  Real-time account view: balance, equity, floating P&L, used and free
  margin, margin level, and all open positions - pulled straight from the
  broker, refreshing every 5 seconds.

Trades
  Every trade with search + filters. Expand any trade to see each
  individual fill. Full chart per trade with your entries, partial TPs
  and stop plotted on real candles, plus bar-by-bar replay.
  Select several trades and group them into one - for scale-ins you added
  too far apart for the automatic grouping to catch.
  Export to CSV or JSON at any time.

Trade log
  A single timeline per trade: every entry, every partial take profit,
  every time you moved your stop (flagged as tightened, moved to
  breakeven, or widened) and every overnight swap charge.

Trade analysis
  MAE / MFE (how much heat you took vs how far it ran), capture % (how
  much of the peak you kept), and a "post-mortem" showing what was left
  on the table after you exited. Plus an exit optimiser that simulates
  fixed R-targets against your actual history to find your best exit.

Calendar & Journal
  Monthly P&L grid. Click any day for its stats, its trades, and a daily
  journal: pre-market plan, notes, lessons, mood, discipline rating and
  a "did I follow the plan?" flag.

Discipline goals
  Set max trades per day, a personal daily-loss cap, a monthly R target
  and minimum checklist adherence. Progress bars on the dashboard, and
  days where you over-traded or broke your cap get flagged on the calendar.

Prop firm rules + alerts
  Per-account daily-loss, max-drawdown (static or trailing) and profit
  targets. Discord alerts when you approach a limit (threshold is yours
  to set), breach one, or hit a profit target - plus an optional daily
  summary posted to Discord.

Prop firm tracker
  Log challenge fees, resets, subscriptions, payouts and refunds per firm.
  See total spent, total received, net profit and return-on-fees, broken
  down by firm and by year. Exports a ledger and a per-year tax summary.

Tags & insights
  Fully configurable tag groups (technical, order flow, liquidity, market
  regime, psychology...). Tag your trades, then drill down: net P&L, win
  rate, expectancy and avg R per tag, with best/worst leaderboards.

Playbooks
  Define strategies with pre-trade rule checklists, then see your win rate
  bucketed by how closely you actually followed them.

Weekly review
  Week-at-a-glance report: stats, per-day results, best and worst trades,
  and the mistakes you logged.

Economic calendar
  High-impact news is pulled automatically and overlaid on your trade
  charts, with a "news during this trade" list and an upcoming-events
  widget on the dashboard.

Backtest sandbox
  Replay historical candles and log practice trades into a separate
  account that's excluded from your real stats.

Accounts
  Multiple accounts (prop challenges, funded, demos) with a global
  aggregated view or per-account drill-down.


UNDER THE HOOD
--------------
  - Automatic sync from cTrader every few minutes.
  - Local candle storage: 1-minute (and higher) price data is captured and
    stored permanently for crypto, indices, gold, forex and oil - so your
    charts still work years later, long after free data sources expire it.
  - Optional broker-exact candles pulled from cTrader itself.
  - Watches your open positions and records stop moves and swap charges
    as they happen.
  - Mobile and tablet friendly, with a collapsible sidebar and hamburger menu.
  - Timezone aware (London by default) - daylight saving handled automatically.
  - Dummy data generator so you can explore the whole app before connecting
    a real account.
  - Manual import from JSON or CSV.
  - Paste screenshots straight from your clipboard onto a trade.
  - Nightly automatic backups.
  - Logs and scheduled-task pages so you can see exactly what's running.
  - Export anything to CSV or JSON.
